feat: add automatic overflow repair to solve text overflow and slide capacity problems

## Problem
The Total-pipe v3 suite generates evidence-based PPTX presentations but fails
to correctly place sufficient content, resulting in widespread text overflow:
- 16 overflow warnings across 9 slides (89% of content slides affected)
- Average 1.78 warnings per slide
- Text cut off and presentations unsuitable for research meetings

Root cause: v26 layout model systematically underestimates vertical space
requirements, generating bounding boxes that are too small for actual content.

## Solution
Implemented automatic content splitting and slide addition:
- Detects text overflow >25% of available space from OfficeCLI warnings
- Automatically splits overflowing content across continuation slides
- Recompiles with fresh v26 layouts for proper spacing
- Preserves paragraph boundaries and narrative context

## How It Works
1. Initial compilation generates layouts and detects overflow
2. Severe overflow (>25% threshold) triggers repair
3. Overflowing text blocks split into 2-4 chunks
4. Continuation slides created (e.g., s04-analysis-cont1)
5. Titles updated to show pagination: "Title (2/3)"
6. Entire deck recompiled with new slide count
7. Fresh layouts generated for all slides including continuations
